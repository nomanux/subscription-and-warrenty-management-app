# 🛡️ Warranty Vault

**Never lose a warranty again.** Warranty Vault is a mobile app that stores all your product warranties in one place, tracks when they expire, and reminds you before they do — so you never miss a claim again.

---

## 💡 The idea

Everyone buys things with warranties — phones, TVs, appliances, furniture, cars — and almost nobody keeps track of them. Receipts get lost, expiry dates are forgotten, and money is wasted when something breaks *just* after a warranty quietly expired.

**Warranty Vault fixes that.** Snap the receipt, save the product, and the app handles the rest: it calculates the expiry date, shows you what's active vs. expiring vs. expired, and reminds you before time runs out.

## 👥 Who it's for

Homeowners, gadget lovers, families — anyone who wants their warranties organized and protected in one simple place.

## ✨ Features

**Available now**
- 📊 **Dashboard** — at-a-glance counts: Total, Active, Expiring Soon, Expired, plus an "Expiring Soon" list
- 🗂️ **Warranty management** — add, edit, and delete products
- ⏱️ **Smart expiry tracking** — auto-calculates the expiry date and a live "days left" countdown
- 🏷️ **Categories** — Electronics, Home Appliances, Kitchen Appliances, Furniture, Vehicle, Others
- 📷 **Receipt photos** — attach a picture of your receipt to each product
- ☁️ **Cloud database** — warranty details are backed up in Firestore
- 📱 **Local receipts** — receipt photos stay on your device
- 🔄 **Auto-updating app** — improvements arrive automatically, no reinstalling

**Coming next**
- 🔐 Secure login (email + Google) so only you can see your data
- 🔔 Expiry reminders (90 / 30 / 7 days before, and on expiry)
- 🔎 Search & filter by category and warranty status
- 🤖 **AI receipt scanner** — snap a receipt and it auto-fills the product name, brand, purchase date, warranty length, serial & model number, and suggests a category

## 📱 Platforms
